{% comment %} "Articles by" section: reverse lookup of posts/activities crediting this profile. Mirrors the forward byline->profile slug-matching logic used in posts.md/activities.md (split subtitle into first+last word, downcase, hyphenate) run backwards against this profile's own slug, PLUS a direct check against profile-link/ profile-link2 overrides -- for the handful of bylines that didn't cleanly slugify (a middle name, an unusual name order) and link here manually instead. Rendered via a single {% raw %}{% capture %}{% endraw %} rather than building a filtered array, since standard Jekyll Liquid has no array-push filter to collect matches into as we go. this_profile_slug is read from page.url, not reconstructed from page.title: the profiles collection's permalink is /profiles/:title/, and Jekyll's :title permalink placeholder is the document's FILENAME slug, not its front-matter title (confirmed: ishita-pinnamaneni.md's title is "Ishita Sai Pinnamaneni", three words -- rebuilding the slug from the first two words of page.title would produce "ishita-sai", which never matches the "Ishita Pinnamaneni" byline her own articles actually use, so her "Articles by" section would silently show nothing even though three real posts correctly link to her profile from the newsletter side.
