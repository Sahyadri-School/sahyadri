Music binds us all. It is something we all have in common, whether one listens to rap songs or instrumental. Music is also a very important form of expression. It can give hope when there is none left. It unites people from all walks of life.

This is why we thought of forming a club to talk about songs which we think are relevant in today's world. Some songs have special connections with us that we want to share with others.

One can suggest or play any songs and then we discuss the meaning. We uncover the multitude of layers. Sometimes we simply engage in old, catchy tunes.

We make the space enjoyable for all.
