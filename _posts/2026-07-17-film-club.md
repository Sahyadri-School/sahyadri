It is held every Sunday in the AV room parallel to the general movie screening time. It is open for senior school students only. We are not told about the film that we will be watching. These movies are slightly different from the whole school movies. They are about social issues, paradoxes, mysteries etc. These films are multi-layered and thought provoking. This, I find, is the USP of Film Club movies. It makes you open your mind and think.

Film Club, for me, has been a place where you can mould your creative self. These movies stay in your memories and keep you wondering about many things around you.
