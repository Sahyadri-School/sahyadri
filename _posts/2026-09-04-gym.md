A dull, tucked-away room on our campus, the school gym once bore little resemblance to a fitness centre. Despite its modest size, it was well-loved and regularly used by the handful of passionate students who knew about its existence. Even so, an upgrade was long overdue.

The enthusiasm for fitness and physical excellence has always burned brightly among the students, and a well-equipped gym is an important facilitator of that growth. Over the past few days, the long-awaited transformation has finally arrived. The gym has doubled in size and been equipped with a range of new equipment. In addition to dumbbells, there are now kettlebells, a multifunctional cable machine and much more.

This development has allowed regular gym-goers to accelerate their progress while encouraging more students to take advantage of the facility. The revamped gym has given this once-overlooked space a new identity and a renewed sense of purpose.
