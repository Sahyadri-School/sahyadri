Mock sessions were introduced last year in Sahyadri for grade 11 and 12 with the objective of giving practice tests for students who will be taking various competitive exams. These exams include JEE Mains, CUET, SAT and several other practice tests. The sessions are held every Sunday in the computer lab from 9:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m.

My personal experience has been very positive. These tests have helped me understand the pattern and nature of subjects like physics, chemistry and mathematics which are very conceptually heavy in these competitive exams. The tests have been productive as they also help me re-visit my 11th-grade syllabus which is necessary for these exams. Through this article I want to inform more students about the importance of these weekly sessions.
