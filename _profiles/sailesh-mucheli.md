I joined Sahyadri in Grade 5 to discover more about myself and learn how I would fare in life without my parents to guide and protect me.

While serving on the Student Council as Head of the IT Committee, I created the school website, Sahyadri Connect. Alongside this role, I started the school's Crochet Club and helped host the senior farewell ceremonies for the 2023–24 and 2025–26 academic years.

I was also heavily involved in organising two major events put on by our batch: the Medieval Fest and the SS Fest. On the creative side, I participated in three school theatre productions, including two Hindi plays, *Vriksh* and *Navras*, and the English play *The Twelve Angry Jurors*.

I also had the opportunity to present a school assembly on the Higgs boson, explaining the basic concepts of subatomic particles to my peers. Outside the classroom and school events, I took part in a school trekking trip to Pandav Patthar, reaching an altitude of 12,500 feet.
