I joined Sahyadri School in Grade 9.

I am an active student who enjoys engaging with campus life and bringing people together through creative pursuits, community initiatives, and collaborative events. Whether performing in a production of *12 Angry Men*, helping run the Cooking Club, or organising and hosting our farewell event, I take pride in being part of a team and contributing to shared goals that bring people closer together.

In addition to my extracurricular activities, I strive to maintain a calm, balanced approach to both my academics and other pursuits. I recognise that consistency and composure often matter as much as enthusiasm in achieving meaningful results.

Unlike other schools, Sahyadri did not feel like a conventional institution where teaching was the primary focus. Instead, I found that the emphasis was on what students were genuinely learning. Life at Sahyadri has felt like a continuous journey of discovery, with new lessons unfolding each day. It is an environment that encourages learning without imposing rigid boundaries, allowing individuals the freedom to grow.

This approach to education resonated with me because I value the opportunity to explore and learn in a supportive and flexible environment.
