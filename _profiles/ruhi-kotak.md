I joined Sahyadri School in Grade 7.

Throughout my time here, there has always been something to do. I have participated in all the plays staged by my batch, from the SS Fest in Grade 7 and the Mughal Fest in Grade 8 to the Mahabharat play in Grade 9 and the Carpe Diem play in Grade 10. In addition, I have given a Bharatanatyam performance and played interschool tennis twice.

I joined Sahyadri mainly because my parents were associated with the school even before I was born and wanted me to study here. The education I received at my previous school did not provide the guidance I needed, so my parents thought it would be better for me to study here. Little did I know that I would learn so much beyond academics.

I must say that my decision to join Sahyadri has been a good one, as the school has provided me with a holistic education that has helped me grow both academically and personally.
