I joined the school in Grade 4 and have been an active member of the school community ever since. Over the years, I have had the opportunity to explore a wide range of interests, from sports and music to performing arts, creativity, and community outreach.

I have been part of the Phoenix Club, where we explore and discuss books, and have participated in several performing arts activities. In Grade 4, I performed in a dance presentation at Freshers’ Eve. I later took part in plays in Grades 8 and 9, including a Hindi play where I introduced the character of Karna. In Grade 11, I contributed to the scriptwriting and performance of another play.

Sports have been an important part of my school life. I play football and volleyball and have also played hockey and table tennis. I have conducted a football camp for younger students and, in Grade 10, helped organise the Sports Fest and Farewell.

I have been playing the tabla for three years and have also been part of the Cooking Club. Through Arts and Crafts and STEAM programmes, I have explored carpentry, pottery, painting, batik, and sustainable projects. One project I particularly enjoyed was building a working model of a washing machine with a peer.

I have also participated in ESD programmes, environmental studies, science fairs, and the Rural Outreach Programme, where we taught children from rural communities. Two Himalayan treks with the school have also been among my most memorable experiences.

As I continue through Grade 11, I look forward to exploring new interests, taking on new challenges, and making the most of the opportunities my school provides.
