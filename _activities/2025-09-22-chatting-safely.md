### What we did in the workshop

In our workshop with students in Grades 6 and 7, we used realistic scenarios reflecting students' online experiences. These scenarios invited students to practise communication strategies, set boundaries, and build community in authentic, relevant contexts. This approach recognises that healthy digital relationships require active care and commitment.

The students practised communication skills to express their emotions clearly and respond constructively to challenging online situations. They:

- Analysed how well they knew the people they interacted with online.
- Identified types of messages that might make someone feel uncomfortable or raise a red flag.
- Used the Feelings & Options thinking routine to analyse and respond to a situation involving a red-flag feeling.
